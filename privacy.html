Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Joes Technologies website handles information when you visit this website or contact us through the contact links provided here.

1. Information collected through this website

The current website is an informational portfolio. It does not provide visitor accounts, an on-site contact form or on-site payment processing. We do not ask you to submit personal information into a form on this website.

Like most websites, the hosting provider and related infrastructure may automatically process technical request information for delivery, security and troubleshooting. Depending on the hosting configuration, this may include an IP address, browser or device details, requested page, referring page and request time. These records are handled by the relevant hosting provider under its own policies.

2. When you contact us

If you choose to email, call or message us using the links on this website, the information you provide is handled through the relevant email, telephone or messaging service. We use the information you voluntarily provide to respond to your enquiry and manage the resulting conversation. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Third-party websites and services

This website contains links to external services and project websites, including WhatsApp, GitHub and other independently operated platforms. When you follow an external link, that service's privacy policy and terms apply. Joes Technologies does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

4. Cookies and analytics

The current website code does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies, and no analytics tracking code is included in the page. Hosting infrastructure, embedded resources or external services may use their own technical storage or cookies when you access them. Browser settings allow you to manage cookies and similar technologies.

5. How information is used and retained

Information you send directly to us is used for the purpose for which you provided it, such as replying to an enquiry or discussing a project. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for that purpose, legitimate business records or applicable legal requirements. Technical server records are subject to the hosting provider's retention practices.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to this informational website and the communications we control. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use the contact links or visit external services. You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent directly to Joes Technologies or to request correction or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate or legal reasons.

8. Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not send sensitive personal information through ordinary email or messaging channels.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
